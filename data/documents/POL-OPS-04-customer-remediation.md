# POL-OPS-04 — Customer Remediation

After confirmed erroneous credits/debits:
1. Freeze affected ledger entries within 30 minutes of detection.
2. Notify Compliance and Customer Care.
3. Reverse duplicates under four-eye approval for amounts > ₹50,000.
4. Issue goodwill communication within T+1.
5. File RBI incident notification if systemic and material.
