# Incident INC-2025-0201 — Card Auth Elevated Declines

Network partner latency caused Apex card auth timeouts. Soft-decline rate rose from 1.2% to 6.8% for 47 minutes. Fallback to secondary network restored success rates. Not classified as payment-failure root-cause cluster (external dependency).
